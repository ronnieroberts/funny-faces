  1. Press Use microphone and choose Allow.
  2. Talk. The bar below moves and so does the mouth.
  3. Press Play and full screen to hide this menu.
Choose Chrome Tab, pick the tab, tick Share tab audio. On a Mac only Chrome tabs can share sound; on Windows a whole screen can too. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer. A TV browser cannot capture sound from another tab; use the computer to capture and let the TV follow.
Voice range and level are per pumpkin. A prepared song (an audio file) has no separate voice ranges, so it plays all together. Press a pumpkin's name to edit its face (Faces tab) and position (Layout tab).
Choosing a face sets it for the pumpkin picked on the Pumpkins tab. A face pack is one JSON file. See docs/FACEPACK.md.

With the menu hidden: drag the face to move it, scroll or pinch to resize. On a remote, the arrows move it; press Select to switch to size mode (up and down resize).
