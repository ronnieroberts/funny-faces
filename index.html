Position and size This face
Hides this menu so you can line faces up on the real thing (projection mapping).
  • Drag the face to move it
  • White diamonds: skew the four corners
  • Blue squares: stretch a side
  • Top dot: rotate. Bottom ring or scroll wheel: zoom
  • Tab: next face. R: straighten. E or Esc: finish
